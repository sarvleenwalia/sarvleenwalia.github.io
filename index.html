01 / Beyond Rote · Community

Research shouldn’t wait until university.

I hated rote learning and wanted school students in India to have a way into research. I started Beyond Rote: a community where high schoolers can explore questions with other students and professors.

My contribution

  • Started the community and led a team of 6+ people from tier 3 cities.
  • Brought professors and schools into student research sessions.
  • Documented sessions so students could revisit the questions and ideas.

Progress so far

  • 2,000+ community members.
  • 5 professors involved and 4+ schools collaborated with.
  • Work with a Delhi University college.

These are my reported community and collaboration counts. Membership is not a measure of active participation or completed research.

03 / Cornershop · MVP in development

Find people who share your taste.

I’m building Cornershop to help creative people connect through shared interests. I want those connections to become conversations, collaborations, and plans to meet.

The first MVP is in development. I report 1,250 waitlist signups; that is interest in the idea, not active users or a shipped product.

Hosting Cornershop Adda is one way I’m exploring the offline side of this idea while I build the app.

04 / Research · Proposed method

Can biological data reveal early signs of neuron degeneration?

I wrote a proposed framework, Machine-Learning Prediction of Stem-Cell Differentiation Fate Using Single-Cell RNA-Seq Data, under Dr Sujata Mohanty, with an application to Parkinson’s dopaminergic neurons.

The proposed workflow uses public single-cell RNA-seq datasets: curate the data, normalize it, cluster cells, test differential gene expression, and examine pathways linked to degeneration.

What exists

A written proposal and planned analysis workflow.

What remains

Run the analysis and evaluate the findings. This is not a validated prediction model, and I am not reporting experimental results.
