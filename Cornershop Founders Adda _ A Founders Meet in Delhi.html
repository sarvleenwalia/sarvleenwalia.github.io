00About

    

      
Find your first believer.

      

        
Cornershop Founders Adda is a Founders Meet in New Delhi for people building things and people curious about starting. The first one ran on 31 October 2026 at First Coffee, Connaught Place.

        
Cornershop is an app in development that connects creative people through shared interests.

      

    

  




    
01How it fits together

    

      

        
        
        
      

      
Start with a conversation.
The first Adda brought people working on their own projects into the same room at First Coffee, Connaught Place.

      
Meet in person.
I hosted the first Founders Meet in Delhi on 31 October 2026. More than 25 people attended, according to my count as organiser.

      
Keep building.
The first meetup is complete. The Cornershop MVP is still in development.
