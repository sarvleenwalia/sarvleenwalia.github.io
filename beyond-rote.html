01The story

I started Beyond Rote so students could do real research.

I went to a high school where nobody was doing research. Every paper felt like it belonged to someone else, and the path into it seemed too difficult to attempt. So I founded a community to find the people I could not find on my own.

We are a research-based community for high schoolers. Students from across India read papers together and discuss research beyond the school syllabus.

Research should not feel out of reach just because no one around you is doing it.

Sarvleen Walia, founder

02Lessons learned

A community is only as durable as its structure.

My biggest mistake as founder was not establishing a clear chain of command. Leadership sat with one person, so when I stepped back the organization struggled to keep moving.

Everything since points to the same fix: shared roles, documented processes, and leaders ready to hand over the work.

03Inside a session

Students read a research paper together, discuss its methods and findings, and ask what the evidence supports and what remains unresolved. We write up the discussion so members can revisit the questions afterward.
